Kaneri Math (Siddhagiri Gramjivan Museum)

Siddhagiri Gurukul Foundation • Vision of Mahatma Gandhi's Self-Reliant Gram Swaraj

The Siddhagiri Gramjivan Museum at Kaneri Math is a sprawling open-air heritage museum located just off the Pune-Bangalore highway near Kolhapur. It brings to life the ancient self-sustaining Indian rural village ecosystem through hundreds of lifelike, life-sized clay sculptures depicting traditional artisans, farmers, blacksmiths, village markets, and Vedic Gurukul systems.
