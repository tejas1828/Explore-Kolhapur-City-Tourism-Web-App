New Palace, Kolhapur (Shahu Museum)

Designed by Major Mant • Built between 1877 and 1884 by Chhatrapati Shahu Maharaj

The New Palace in Kolhapur is an extraordinary example of Indo-Saracenic royal architecture, constructed with finely polished black stone. Spanning seven years of construction, it features an eight-angled clock tower, serene gardens, and a private lake. The ground floor houses the world-famous Shree Chhatrapati Shahu Museum, presenting rare Maratha armaments, swords, royal costumes, gold-embroidered robes, and vintage photographs.
