Rankala Lake

Rankala Lake, Kolhapur

Historic 9th-Century Reservoir • The Heartbeat of Kolhapur's Evening Culture

Rankala Lake is a breathtaking freshwater lake situated in western Kolhapur, constructed during the reign of the 9th-century kings. Legend holds that a stone quarry existed here before an earthquake opened a perennial subterranean spring. Today, the lake's picturesque Chaupati promenade is surrounded by the Sandhya Math, Shalini Palace, boating jetties, and famous local culinary treats including genuine Kolhapuri Misal and Bhel.
