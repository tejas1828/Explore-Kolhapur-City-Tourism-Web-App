Kopeshwar Temple

Kopeshwar Temple, Khidrapur (Kolhapur)

12th-Century Shilahara Dynasty Marvel • Famous Circular Open-Sky Swargamandap

Nestled serenely along the sacred Krishna river at Khidrapur near Kolhapur, Kopeshwar Temple is a peerless masterpiece of ancient Indian stone carving. Built by the Shilahara kings in the 12th century, it is world-renowned for its circular Swargamandap (Heavenly Hall) supported by 48 intricately carved stone pillars with an open circular ceiling that captures celestial starlight. Uniquely, the temple does not have a Nandi pavilion directly facing the sanctum.
