Mahalakshmi Temple

Shree Mahalakshmi Temple (Ambabai), Kolhapur

Built by the Chalukya King Karnadeva in 634 CE • Revered Shakti Peetha

Mahalakshmi Temple (also known as Ambabai Mandir) is one of the most revered Hindu temples in India, dedicated to Goddess Mahalakshmi, worshipped affectionately by locals as Ambabai. The temple is an architectural marvel of stone carving with its intricate mandapas, Garuda stambha, and the celestial Kiranotsav phenomenon where the sun rays illuminate the deity's idol. Visiting Kolhapur Mahalakshmi is considered essential for completing the sacred Venkateswara yatra to attain spiritual peace.
