Bhavani Mandap

Bhavani Mandap, Kolhapur

The Historic Royal Court of the Maratha Rulers • Seat of Chhatrapati Shahu Maharaj

Bhavani Mandap is a grand historical monument situated at the heart of Kolhapur, adjacent to the Mahalakshmi Temple. Historically serving as the city's royal court and administrative nucleus, it was the center of governance, cultural galas, and political assemblies. Today, visitors can admire vintage taxidermy displays, historic royal carriages, statues, and rich Maratha-British Raj architectural craftsmanship that echoes the glory of the princely state.
