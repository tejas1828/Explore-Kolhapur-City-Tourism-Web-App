Panhala Fort

Panhala Fort, Kolhapur

Strategically Perched in the Sahyadris • Witness to Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj's Heroic Feats

Panhala Fort is one of the largest and most magnificent forts in the Deccan, rising dramatically above the pass that connects coastal Maharashtra with the interior plateaus. Closely intertwined with the heroic saga of Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj and Shiva Kashid's supreme sacrifice, the fort boasts legendary historic landmarks including the Teen Darwaza, the Ambarkhana granary, the Andhar Bavadi (hidden well), and Sajja Kothi with breathtaking views.
