Jotiba Temple

Shree Jotiba Temple, Kolhapur (Wadi Ratnagiri)

Perched at 3,124 feet above sea level • Built in 1730 by Ranoji Shinde

Perched atop the picturesque Wadi Ratnagiri hills approximately 18 km northwest of Kolhapur, Shree Jotiba Temple is dedicated to Lord Jyotiba, revered as an incarnation of the holy Trinity (Brahma, Vishnu, and Mahesha). Famous for the grand Chaitra Poornima fair where devotees shower pink gulal across the mountain, the temple offers breathtaking vistas of green valleys, cool mountain breezes, and profound spiritual tranquility.
